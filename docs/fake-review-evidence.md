# Evidence of fake reviews in today's apps

Researched 2026-10-29. Live version: https://claude.ai/artifact/JvdePcRg97XVD3eCNTaQQM

## Summary

Fake reviews are real, large and still getting through on every major app. The strongest numbers:

- **292 million** reviews blocked or removed by Google Maps in 2025 alone, up from 240 million in 2024.
- **54%** of the fraud Tripadvisor caught in 2024 came from businesses boosting themselves.
- **About 16%** of Yelp restaurant reviews were filtered as suspicious (Harvard study), with small independent restaurants the most likely to fake.
- **28,886** fake Naver Place reviews for 707 businesses, including restaurants, made by one Busan ring using bought accounts and recycled receipts (police, July 2026).
- **108,709** undisclosed paid posts caught by Korea's Fair Trade Commission from 2021 to 2025.
- New laws in the **US (2024)** and **UK (2025)** now fine fake reviews directly, up to $51,744 per violation or 10% of global turnover.

## Platform transparency reports

The platforms' own reports are the strongest evidence, because they count only the fakes they caught. Google Maps alone blocked or removed 292 million reviews in 2025, and the ones that slip through are by definition not in these numbers.

| Platform | Period | Key number | Detail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Google Maps](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-maps-spam-fighting-2025-41176.html) | 2025 (reported Apr 2026) | 292M policy-violating reviews blocked or removed | Up about 21% on 2024's 240M; 13M fake Business Profiles removed; 783,000+ accounts restricted |
| [Google Maps](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-maps-spam-fighting-2024-39186.html) | 2024 | 240M reviews blocked or removed | 12M fake Business Profiles removed |
| [Yelp](https://www.yelp-press.com/press-releases/press-release-details/2025/Yelp-Releases-2024-Trust--Safety-Report/default.aspx) | 2024 (reported 5 Feb 2025) | 18% of ~21M reviews not recommended by its filter | 120 paid-review alerts and 427 suspicious-activity alerts on ~550 businesses; 1,300+ accounts closed for review-exchange rings |
| [Tripadvisor](https://tripadvisor.mediaroom.com/2025-03-18-Tripadvisors-2025-Transparency-Report-reveals-strong-review-submissions-and-improved-fraud-detection) | 2024 (reported 18 Mar 2025) | 2.7M fraudulent reviews caught of 31.1M submitted (about 8.7%, our calculation) | 54% of fraud was boosting by owners, staff or affiliates; 214,000 AI-written reviews removed; 360,000 tied to staff incentive schemes |
| [Amazon](https://www.retaildive.com/news/amazon-fight-fake-reviews/736089) | 2023 (reported Jan 2025) | 250M+ suspected fake reviews blocked | Sued the broker ReviewServiceUSA.com jointly with the Better Business Bureau |

Tripadvisor's finding matters most for Told by Mouth: more than half of its fraud came from the business itself, which is exactly what an in-person, real-name review makes hard.

## Academic studies

Peer-reviewed work shows that fake reviews are common, that they work, and that small independent restaurants are the most tempted to use them.

- [Luca and Zervas, "Fake It Till You Make It"](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:hbs:wpaper:14-006) (Harvard Business School, 2015): about **16% of Yelp restaurant reviews** were filtered as suspicious. Independent restaurants with few or recently bad reviews were the most likely to post fake praise, and fake negative reviews rose with local competition.
- [He, Hollenbeck and Proserpio, "The Market for Fake Reviews"](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/964346) (Marketing Science, Sep 2022): tracked about **1,500 Amazon products** buying fake reviews through private social media groups. Fake reviews raised ratings and sales in the short term; once sellers stopped, ratings fell and one-star reviews rose.
- [EU sweep](https://www.wired-gov.net/wg/news.nsf/print/Protecting+consumers+from+misleading+reviews+55+of+screened+websites+violate+EU+law+21012022103800) (Jan 2022, listed under Regulators): authorities doubted review authenticity on **two thirds of 223 major sites**.

## Korea: Naver, Kakao Map, Baemin and paid blogs

Korea has the clearest restaurant-specific evidence. Naver's receipt check was meant to prove a real visit, and a whole industry now buys and recycles receipts to get around it. Newest first.

| Case | Date | What happened | Key number |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [KFTC hidden-ad monitoring](https://biz.sbs.co.kr/amp/article/20000341151) (SBS Biz) | 21 Oct 2026 | KFTC figures given to the National Assembly on "내돈내산" posts that were really paid ads | 108,709 posts caught 2021 to 2025 (17,020 / 21,037 / 25,966 / 22,011 / 22,675); food was 9.8% in 2025 |
| [Busan police "review factory"](https://www.wikitree.co.kr/articles/1147441) (Wikitree) | 20 Jul 2026 | Ad agency used hijacked Naver accounts bought on Telegram, owners' receipts and paid reviewers, plus macro clicks to lift rankings | 28,886 fake Naver Place reviews for 707 businesses (incl. restaurants and cafes) over 5 years; ₩1.9bn earned; 24 people referred to prosecutors |
| [Fake review price list](https://www.mt.co.kr/society/2025/12/30/2025123011512149448) (Money Today) | 30 Dec 2025 | Agencies sell restaurant reviews on Naver and Google, drip-fed 1 or 2 a day to avoid detection; owners also swap reviews in group chats | Up to ₩7,000 per review; one consultant fined ₩10M for selling reviews at ₩1,800 each |
| [Naver "내돈내산" blog reviews on the map](https://v.daum.net/v/20250813170037061?f=p) | 13 Aug 2025 | Naver started surfacing only purchase-verified blog reviews on Naver Place | KFTC found 22,011 hidden-ad posts in 2024; 9,423 (42.8%) on Naver blogs, second only to Instagram |
| [Political rating attacks](https://www.wikitree.co.kr/articles/1035694) (Wikitree) | 23 Mar 2025 | During the impeachment protests, lists labelled restaurants "left-wing" and they got one-star ratings unrelated to their food | 9 restaurants targeted; attacks visible on Kakao Map |
| [Receipt-review chat rooms](https://v.daum.net/v/6BJlm5qyWk?f=p) (Hankyung) | 2 Sep 2024 | KakaoTalk open chats hand out real receipts and food photos so members can post receipt reviews for restaurants they never visited | ₩500 per receipt review on Naver or Kakao Map, ₩1,000 per booked-visit review; 200+ members in one room |
| [Baemin legal action](https://letter.wepick.kr/post/2047) (Wepick) | 15 Oct 2024 | Woowa Brothers has sued review-manipulation and fake-order sellers since 2018 | 11 businesses fined or jailed, 12 more under investigation; ~₩10,000 per fake review |

The Busan and Hankyung cases matter most for Told by Mouth: they show that a receipt, a photo and a logged-in account can all be bought, so none of them alone proves a person was there.

## What this means for Told by Mouth

Every check today's apps rely on can be bought: accounts (Telegram), receipts (KakaoTalk chats), photos (supplied by the owner) and even visit bookings. Told by Mouth's answer is the one thing these rings cannot fake at scale, a named person standing in front of you.

- Owner self-boosting (54% on Tripadvisor) needs anonymous accounts; a real-name review handed over in person removes them.
- Review bombing and extortion need remote posting; face-to-face sharing makes a remote one-star attack impossible.
- Receipt and photo checks are not enough on their own, which is a caution for our own "photograph your food" step: it should be one signal, not the proof.

**Caveats.** Platform numbers count what each platform caught, using its own definitions, so they are not comparable with each other. Yelp's 16% is reviews its filter flagged, not proven fakes. The Baemin figures come from a 2024 newsletter citing a 2023 report. Sources were opened and read on 29 Oct 2026.
